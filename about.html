Current Research

Role-Dependent Use of Mixed-Quality Data in Offline RL

Method: Trust-Mix | Ongoing research

Submission status: Abstract submitted to ICLR 2027.

Investigating when transitions generated by low-quality behavior can support value learning and policy improvement without treating their recorded actions as equally useful targets for imitation.

  • Developing Trust-Mix, an offline reinforcement learning approach that separates three roles of data: learning action values from transitions, improving the policy at recorded states using those values, and directly supervising the policy with recorded actions.
  • Retaining the full dataset for value learning and value-guided policy improvement while weighting recorded actions used for behavioral supervision.
  • Studying datasets containing a small expert-policy component and a substantially larger random-policy component, without providing the learner with source identities.
  • Using paired expert-only and mixed-data experiments, data-role controls, and component ablations to examine when low-quality data are useful and which learning roles account for their effects.
